## Strict Compliance Guidelines For All Web Content

> **1. NO ABSOLUTE CLAIMS:** Words like "100% guarantee", "Cure", or "Risk-Free" are prohibited.
> **2. SUCCESS RATE DISCLAIMERS:** Anytime IVF or surgery success rates are mentioned, the following disclaimer MUST be present in italicized subscript: *"Success rates vary significantly based on patient age, medical history, and specific diagnostic factors."*

## Global FAQ Dataset (Structured for Schema Markup)

### General Gynecology FAQs
**Q: When should I first visit a gynecologist?**
A: It is recommended that reproductive-aged women visit a gynecologist annually for a well-woman exam. If you experience severe pelvic pain, irregular bleeding, or suspect an infection, schedule an appointment immediately. 

**Q: Are fibroids dangerous?**
A: Uterine fibroids are typically benign (non-cancerous) tumors. While not inherently dangerous or life-threatening, they can cause heavy bleeding, pain, and fertility issues that greatly impact quality of life and may require medical or surgical intervention.

### Surgical FAQs
**Q: How long does it take to recover from a laparoscopic hysterectomy?**
A: Recovery from a minimally invasive laparoscopic hysterectomy is generally faster than abdominal surgery. Most patients return to light activities within 2 to 4 weeks. *Expected recovery times are averages; individual recovery will vary.*

**Q: Will I have a large scar after myomectomy?**
A: If a laparoscopic or robotic myomectomy is performed, the incisions are very small (tiny "keyhole" incisions). Only an open abdominal myomectomy leaves a larger scar. Dr. Agarwal will discuss the most appropriate minimally invasive approach for your specific case.

### Pregnancy & Fertility FAQs
**Q: What makes a pregnancy high-risk?**
A: A pregnancy is considered high-risk if there are potential complications that could affect the mother, baby, or both. Factors include maternal age (over 35), pre-existing medical conditions (like high blood pressure or diabetes), or carrying multiples (twins/triplets).

**Q: What is the success rate of IVF on the first try?**
A: IVF success rates depend heavily on maternal age and the underlying cause of infertility. Generally, women under 35 have the highest success rates per cycle. *Success rates vary significantly based on individual medical conditions. Please consult with the fertility specialist to understand your realistic chances.*
