Laparoscopic Ovarian Cystectomy (Cyst Removal Surgery)

A safe, minimally invasive approach to remove painful ovarian cysts while preserving your ovarian healthy tissue and fertility.

What is a Laparoscopic Cystectomy?

An ovarian cystectomy is a surgical procedure to remove a cyst from your ovary. In a laparoscopic approach, Dr. Agarwal makes just a few tiny incisions (keyholes) in your abdomen. Using a specialized camera and miniature surgical tools, she carefully separates the cyst from the healthy ovarian tissue and removes it.

This approach is heavily utilized for complex cysts such as dermoid cysts, large endometriomas (associated with Endometriosis), or cysts that appear suspicious on an ultrasound.

When is Surgery Necessary?

Many ovarian cysts resolve on their own without intervention. However, surgery is recommended if a cyst is:

  • Larger than 5 centimeters in diameter
  • Causing severe, persistent pelvic pain
  • Suspected to be an endometrioma or dermoid cyst
  • Posing a high risk for ovarian torsion (twisting of the ovary, a medical emergency)

Understanding the Procedure

The surgery is performed under general anesthesia. It typically takes between 1 to 2 hours. Because the incisions are so small, there is significantly less trauma to the abdominal wall compared to an open surgery (laparotomy).

Recovery Timeline

One of the greatest benefits of laparoscopic surgery is a rapid recovery.

  • Hospital Stay: Most patients go home the same day or the morning after.
  • First Few Days: You will experience some abdominal soreness and shoulder pain (caused by the gas used to inflate the abdomen during surgery).
  • Return to Work: Most women return to desk jobs or light activities within 1 to 2 weeks.

*Medical information is for educational purposes only. Recovery times vary heavily based on individual condition. Consult a qualified doctor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my ovary be removed along with the cyst?

In almost all cases, the primary goal of an ovarian cystectomy is "fertility preservation." Dr. Agarwal uses advanced techniques to strip the cyst wall away while leaving the healthy ovary intact. Removal of the entire ovary (oophorectomy) is only performed if absolutely medically necessary.

Can the cyst come back after surgery?

Yes, certain types of cysts, like endometriomas, have a higher recurrence rate. We often use medical management, like hormonal birth control, post-surgery to suppress new cyst formation.
