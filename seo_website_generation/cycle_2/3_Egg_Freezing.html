Why Consider Egg Freezing?

Female fertility naturally declines with age, significantly accelerating after age 35. Egg freezing gives you the flexibility to delay childbearing to focus on your career, finding the right partner, or undergoing medical treatments (like chemotherapy or severe Endometriosis care) that might impact your fertility.

The Step-by-Step Procedure

The process mirrors the first half of IVF Treatment:

  1. Ovarian Reserve Testing: Blood tests (AMH) and ultrasounds determine the health and quantity of your eggs.
  2. Ovarian Stimulation: 10-14 days of hormone injections to stimulate the ovaries to mature multiple eggs.
  3. Egg Retrieval: A 15-minute, minimally invasive procedure under sedation to collect the eggs using a transvaginal ultrasound needle.
  4. Vitrification (Freezing): Using a rapid-freeze technique, eggs are frozen to sub-zero temperatures, preventing ice crystals from forming, preserving them indefinitely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of egg freezing in Kolkata?

Costs involve the initial retrieval cycle (medications, anesthesia, procedure) and a yearly storage fee for the frozen eggs. We encourage patients to book a Preconception Assessment so we can provide a detailed, completely transparent financial breakdown based on your lab protocol.

Is egg freezing 100% guaranteed to result in a baby?

No procedure is fully guaranteed. Frozen eggs must survive the thaw, successfully fertilize, and develop into healthy embryos down the line. However, freezing a sufficient number of high-quality eggs at a younger age drastically improves your statistical probability of success.
