Diagnostic & Operative Hysteroscopy in Kolkata

A highly precise, zero-incision procedure to look directly inside your uterus to diagnose and treat bleeding abnormalities or fertility issues.

What is a Hysteroscopy?

A hysteroscopy is a procedure where Dr. Agarwal uses a hysteroscope — a thin, lighted tube with a camera — to examine the inside of your cervix and uterus. Because the scope is inserted through the vagina, no abdominal incisions are required.

When is it Recommended?

This procedure is a critical tool for both diagnosing (Diagnostic) and treating (Operative) uterine conditions:

  • Investigating abnormal uterine bleeding or post-menopausal bleeding.
  • Removing uterine polyps or small submucosal Fibroids.
  • Diagnosing causes of repeated miscarriages or part of an Infertility Workup.
  • Locating a displaced IUD.

Understanding the Procedure

The procedure is often performed as a day-case surgery under light anesthesia or local anesthesia. During an operative hysteroscopy, tiny instruments are passed through the hysteroscope to remove polyps or fibroids immediately, saving you from a second surgery.

Recovery Timeline

Recovery is exceptionally fast. Most patients go home within an hour or two of the procedure. You may experience mild cramping and light bleeding for a few days, similar to a menstrual period. Normal activities can usually be resumed within 1 to 2 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a hysteroscopy painful?

Most patients report only mild cramping during recovery. Since the procedure is usually done under sedation or anesthesia, you will not feel pain during the operation itself.

Do I need this before IVF?

A diagnostic hysteroscopy is frequently recommended prior to IVF Treatment to ensure the uterine cavity is healthy and optimized for embryo implantation.
