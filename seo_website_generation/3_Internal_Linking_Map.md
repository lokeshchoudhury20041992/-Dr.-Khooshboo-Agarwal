# Contextual Internal Linking Strategy Map

*Prepared by: Internal Linking Strategist Agent*

The site relies on a Hub and Spoke structure (Silo architecture) to pass PageRank to conversion pages and establish Topical Authority.

## Linking Rules Enforced:
1. Every child page points back to its parent hub using exact-match anchor text.
2. Sibling pages interlink contextually (e.g., PCOS links to Infertility).
3. Every condition page must link to its corresponding potential surgery page.
