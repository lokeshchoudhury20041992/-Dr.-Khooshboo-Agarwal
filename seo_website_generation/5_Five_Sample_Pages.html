Understanding Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)

PCOS is a common hormonal disorder. Symptoms include irregular menstrual cycles, excess hair growth, acne, and weight gain.

How is PCOS Diagnosed?

Diagnosis typically involves blood tests, a pelvic exam, and a pelvic ultrasound to check for cysts on the ovaries.

Our Treatment Approach

We believe in a holistic approach combining medical management and lifestyle changes to improve your quality of life. If you are struggling to conceive, PCOS management is a critical first step in our Infertility Workup.

What is a Laparoscopic Hysterectomy?

A laparoscopic hysterectomy is a minimally invasive surgical procedure to remove the uterus using small "keyhole" incisions. We often recommend this for severe Uterine Fibroids or advanced Endometriosis.

What Constitutes a High-Risk Pregnancy?

A high-risk pregnancy means you or your baby might be at increased risk of health problems before, during, or after delivery.

IVF Treatment (In Vitro Fertilization)

Comprehensive IVF treatment with advanced reproductive technology. Step-by-step guidance for your journey to parenthood.

The Step-by-Step IVF Process

  1. Ovarian Stimulation: Using medications to produce multiple eggs.
  2. Egg Retrieval: A minor surgical procedure to collect eggs.
  3. Fertilization: Often utilizing IVF or ICSI techniques.
  4. Embryo Transfer: Placing the embryo into the uterus.

*IVF success rates vary significantly based on patient age, medical history, and specific diagnostic factors. There is no 100% guarantee of pregnancy. Please consult with our specialists to review your specific statistical probabilities.
